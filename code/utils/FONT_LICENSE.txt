Bundled fonts: TeX Gyre Heros
==============================

Files:
  texgyreheros-regular.otf
  texgyreheros-bold.otf
  texgyreheros-italic.otf
  texgyreheros-bolditalic.otf

TeX Gyre Heros is a Helvetica-metric-compatible sans-serif font produced by
the GUST e-foundry. It is used here in place of Helvetica so that publication
figures can be reproduced without shipping a proprietary, non-redistributable
font, while keeping glyph metrics (and therefore figure layout) unchanged.

Source (upstream):
  https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros
  Mirror used for download: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre/opentype/
